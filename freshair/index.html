Quick answers

Is focus really free forever?

Yes. Unlimited focus sessions stay free. Pro unlocks extras — sounds, custom breaks & rounds, every companion, richer history, and backup.

What are Friends and gems?

Complete focus sessions to earn gems, then claim doodle companions. Pro can unlock every companion instantly.

Which Pro plan should I pick?
