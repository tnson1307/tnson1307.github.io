FAQ

Quick answers

Is focus really free forever?

Yes. Unlimited focus sessions, every focus mode (Strict, Loose, Whitelist), one Focus hours window, full history and charts, backup, mood check-in and one custom slime stay free. Pro adds more Focus hours, a squad of up to 3 slimes, more sounds, custom breaks & rounds and exclusive friends. Other companions still need gems from focus.

What are Focus hours?

Set daily time windows and pick the apps that distract you. During a window those apps stay locked, even when Nomodoro is closed. Finish a focus session and they open for exactly your break, then lock again. Free: one window every day, up to 3 apps. Pro: up to 5 windows on the days you choose, any number of apps and whole categories. It uses Apple’s Screen Time API, and your choices never leave your iPhone.

How does Whitelist mode block apps?

It uses Apple’s Screen Time API. You pick up to 10 apps that stay allowed; every other app is shielded only while a focus session runs, and released when it ends. Your app choices never leave your iPhone.

Can I quit a session early?

Yes — hold End for 3 seconds. Your buddy gets a little sad while you hold, and cheers if you let go. A count-up session just asks to save.

What are Friends and gems?

Complete focus sessions to earn gems, then claim doodle companions. Pro unlocks five exclusives (bubble tea, donut, puppy, capybara, apple) without gems — everyone else still needs enough gems to claim.

Which Pro plan should I pick?

Try Monthly if you want flexibility. Yearly is the best value (~58% less than paying month-to-month) and starts with a 7-day free trial — we remind you 2 days before it ends. Lifetime is one payment if you know you’ll stay.
