Pocket Rook

Chess at the table. No internet.

Play chess with the person across from you — one phone between you, or two phones that connect directly and securely. No account, no game server, no Wi‑Fi needed.

  • One phone, two players — the board turns to face each side
  • Two phones joined by a QR scan or six-digit code
  • Synced clocks, one-tap rematch, shareable final position
  • Swift
  • Peer-to-peer
  • iOS 17+

Layerly

Morning and night, in the right order.

Keep an honest shelf and a calmer routine. AM and PM stacks stay separate so serums never jump the line — glow check-ins stay on your device.

  • Guided player walks each layer in sequence
  • Shelf notes, PAO, expiry, and refill nudges
  • Optional progress photos stored on-device
  • Swift
  • Local-first
  • iOS

Nomodoro

Focus · Rest · Repeat.

A calm visual pomodoro with doodle companions and a slime squad you make yourself. One clear task, a gentle countdown, kind rounds for minds that need less noise. Unlimited focus stays free.

  • Shape your own slime; it melts or bounces with your mood check-in
  • Strict, Whitelist, or Loose — block every app but the ones you allow
  • Widgets and Live Activities on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island
  • Swift
  • Screen Time API
  • Live Activities

HomeLog

Warranties, repairs, and rooms — quietly.

A structure for what you own: Space → Room → Asset. Log purchase dates, service visits, and spend. Reminders land before something fails. No account to start.

  • Houses, apartments, and offices as spaces
  • Purchase vs maintenance totals per property
  • Recurring maintenance on the lock screen
  • Swift
  • Local-first
  • Offline

CoupleBites

Every bite, together.

Snap what you eat and it turns into a sticker. Your partner sees it right away, and every meal feeds the slime you raise together. No account, no server — just your own iCloud. No calorie counting, promise.

  • Invite your partner with a link from any chat app
  • React with 😋 🤤 ❤️ 🔥 😆 and keep a streak with forgiving freezes
  • A monthly recap with your stickers on a globe you can spin
  • SwiftUI
  • CloudKit
  • Vision
Visit CoupleBites →

ShipCheck

Stamp it cleared before Apple stamps it rejected.

A Mac app that reads your real Xcode project and live App Store Connect data — then lists what will bounce the submission, on your machine, before review does.

  • Diffs IAP product IDs, Privacy Manifest, and age-rating answers
  • Keys stay in Keychain; no ShipCheck server, ever
  • CLI for CI so drift fails the build, not the submit
  • Swift
  • macOS
  • App Store Connect API
Visit ShipCheck →
