Why Layerly

A full shelf is not a routine.

Most of us don’t lack products — we lack a calm way to use them in the right order, every morning and night.

Toner first or serum first? Which bottles are AM? Which are already empty? Layerly was built for that exact moment at the sink.

Separate day and night stacks. Guided layering so you don’t mix steps. A shelf that remembers what you own, when it expires, and how your glow looks over time — with photos that stay on your device.

How it works

Three quiet steps.

No clinical diagnosis. No overwhelm. Just a clearer habit you can actually keep.

  1. Build your shelf

    Add cleansers, toners, serums, moisturizers, SPF, and more — with brand, notes, photos, and PAO or expiry dates.

  2. Set AM & PM stacks

    Layerly sorts products into the right order. Start the player and move through each step without second-guessing.

  3. Stay consistent

    Mark slots done, keep streaks, set gentle reminders, and optionally snap a daily glow check-in for your timeline.

Features

Built for real routines.

Everything you need to stop abandoning skincare by Wednesday — and still keep the habit feeling light.

  • Guided layering

    A step-by-step AM/PM player so you never skip a layer or mix actives in the wrong order.

  • Honest shelf

    Track what you own, archive empties, restore when you repurchase, and avoid buying duplicates.

  • PAO & expiry

    Gentle refill and expiry awareness so bottles don’t quietly go stale in the cabinet.

  • Glow check-ins

    Optional daily progress photos for your on-device timeline — not facial recognition or skin diagnosis.

  • Streaks & calendar

    See full days, partial days, and misses at a glance on Home and Stats — so consistency feels visible.

  • Reminders & widgets

    AM/PM reminders at times you choose, plus widgets and Live Activity cues while you layer.
