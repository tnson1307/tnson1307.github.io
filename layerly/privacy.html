Privacy Policy

Layerly (iOS) · Effective date: August 14, 2026 · Last updated: August 14, 2026

Layerly (“we”, “us”, “the App”) is a skincare routine companion. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used. By using Layerly, you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

Layerly is provided as a consumer iOS application (bundle ID com.tnson.layerly.app). For privacy questions, contact: tnson@bwaco.vn or open an issue on GitHub.

2. Information the App stores

Layerly is designed to keep your routine data primarily on your device. Depending on how you use the App, this may include:

  • Product shelf details you enter (name, brand, step, usage slot, notes, PAO / expiry dates).
  • Routine completion history and streak-related preferences.
  • Optional product photos and optional daily progress / check-in photos you capture or choose.
  • App preferences (language, reminders, skin profile settings you choose).
  • Backup files you export or import (including optional iCloud Drive copies you enable).

Photos and product data are stored in the App’s local storage / Application Support on your device (and in any backup destination you explicitly choose).

3. Permissions

  • Camera — to photograph products or progress check-ins, only when you use those features.
  • Photo Library — to let you pick product images you choose to attach.
  • Notifications — optional reminders / expiry-related alerts if you enable them.
  • iCloud Drive (optional) — only if you use cloud backup features available to Pro users.

4. Purchases

Optional Layerly Pro purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit / your Apple ID. We do not receive your full payment card details. Apple’s privacy terms also apply to purchase processing.

5. Analytics, advertising, and selling data

Layerly does not sell your personal information. The App is not built around third-party advertising SDKs. We do not operate a separate Layerly account server that collects your shelf for marketing.

6. Children

Layerly is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). Do not use the App if you are under the applicable age.

7. Data retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until you delete it in-app (for example archiving/removing products), clear App data, or delete the App. Exported backup files remain wherever you saved them until you delete those files.

8. International users

Because data is primarily on-device, processing generally occurs on your iPhone. If you export backups to cloud services you control (for example iCloud), those providers’ terms also apply.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact

Privacy requests: tnson@bwaco.vn
