Privacy Policy

HomeLog – Home Inventory
Effective date: August 4, 2026
Developer: Ngoc Son Tran

This Privacy Policy explains how HomeLog (“the App”) handles information. HomeLog is a home inventory app. Most data stays on your device.

1. Information we store on your device

The App stores the following locally on your device (for example in an on-device database and app settings):

  • Property, room, and asset details you enter
  • Maintenance logs, reminders, and related notes
  • Optional display name, language, theme, and avatar preferences
  • A local flag for Premium access status (synced with the store when online)

We do not operate a HomeLog account system. If you delete the App, local data is removed from that device unless you have your own backup.

2. Purchases and subscriptions

HomeLog offers optional Premium subscriptions through the Apple App Store. Payments are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to verify purchase entitlements so Premium features can unlock on your device.

Apple and RevenueCat may process purchase-related identifiers and transaction data according to their own policies:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App may send local reminders you configure (for example maintenance due dates). Notification permission is optional.

4. Data we do not sell

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use third-party advertising SDKs in HomeLog.

5. Children’s privacy

HomeLog is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the App if you are under 13.

6. How to delete your data

Because data is stored on your device, you can delete it by deleting the App from your device. To manage or cancel a Premium subscription, use Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your Apple device.

7. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above will be revised when changes are posted.
