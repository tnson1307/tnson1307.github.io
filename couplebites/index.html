What's inside

Built for two people who like to know what the other one had for lunch.

📸

Every bite, a sticker

Take a photo and the dish is cut out into a sticker on your phone. Breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks. No typing needed.

💌

One diary for two

Invite your partner with a link you can send from any chat app. Whatever either of you logs shows up for both, through your own iCloud.

🫧

Raise a slime together

Every day you log, your shared slime grows a little, and days you both log count extra. Name it and dress it up with hats, glasses and more.

BabyKid 7Grown 60Legend 180Mythic 365
😋

React to their meals

Hold a photo of your partner's meal to send 😋 🤤 ❤️ 🔥 or 😆. They get a little notification.

🔥

Keep a streak

A daily streak, with streak freezes that forgive the odd missed day.

🎞️

Monthly recap

At the end of each month, watch a short story of everything you ate, with your stickers on a globe you can spin.

Free & Pro

Everything you need as a couple is free. Pro adds your full history and a few extras.

Free

Forever
  • Unlimited meal logging and stickers
  • Shared diary and sync with your partner
  • Shared slime, reactions and streaks
  • This month's recap
  • Home Screen widget
  • iCloud sync and manual backup (export/import)
  • The last 30 days of history

Pro

Monthly, yearly or lifetime
  • Your full history, every day since the start
  • Every past recap, plus sharing recap cards
  • Place tags on meals
  • Pro-only slime accessories and avatar icons
  • 3 streak freezes instead of 1

Prices are shown in the app and on the App Store before you buy. Cancel any time in your Apple ID settings.

🔒

Private by design

No account, no sign-up, no ads, no trackers. Your diary stays on your phone and in your own iCloud, shared only with the partner you invite. We never see it. Read the privacy policy.
