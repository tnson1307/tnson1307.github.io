How it works

Three steps, no sign-up. Start on your own and invite your partner whenever you like.

1

Snap your meal

Take a photo or pick one from your library. The app cuts the dish out of the background right on your phone and tells food from drinks. Breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks each get their own place.

2

Invite your partner

Send your invite link from Messages, WhatsApp, Zalo, Messenger or any other chat app. One tap on their iPhone and you share one diary, synced through iCloud.

3

Watch it add up

Every day you log feeds your shared slime. React to each other's meals, mark the ones you ate together, and get a short story of your month when it ends.

Quick answers

The things couples ask most.

How do I connect with my partner?

Tap the partner button on Home and share your invite link from any chat app, like Messages, WhatsApp, Zalo or Messenger. Your partner opens the link on their iPhone and CoupleBites joins your diary. Pasting the link into the app works too. You both need CoupleBites on an iPhone signed in to iCloud. Once connected, every meal either of you logs shows up for both, and their earlier meals move into the shared diary.

Does it work for long-distance couples?

Yes. Your diary syncs through iCloud, so it doesn't matter where each of you is. You see each other's meals as they come in, react to them, and get a little notification when your partner logs a meal or reacts to yours.

Where is our diary stored, and can you see it?

Your diary lives on your phone and in your own iCloud, shared only with the partner you invite. There is no account, no sign-up and no server of ours, and there are no ads, analytics or trackers. We never receive or see your meals or photos. If you're not signed in to iCloud, everything stays on your phone.

What is the slime, and how does it grow?

It's a little jelly cat you raise together. It gains a level for every day with a meal logged, and days you both log count extra. It grows from Baby to Kid at level 7, Grown at 60, Legend at 180 and Mythic at 365. Accessories unlock as it levels up, and a few are Pro only. Give it a name and dress it up however you like.

Does CoupleBites count calories?

No. There are no calories, macros, weights or goals, and you never type what a dish is. It's a photo diary of what you ate, made to share with your partner, not a diet tracker.

What does Pro add?

Your full history (the free plan shows the last 30 days), every past monthly recap and sharing recap cards, a map of every place you have eaten, a widget with your partner's latest meal, On this day memories, a printable monthly scrapbook, unlimited places to try (free keeps 3), milestone reminders with a days together widget, and Pro-only slime accessories. Logging, the shared diary, the slime, reactions, iCloud sync and backup stay free.

Do we both need Pro?

Pro belongs to the Apple ID that buys it, so each partner's plan is their own. If you're in the same Family Sharing group, one purchase can cover both of you.

How do I cancel or restore Pro?

Cancel a subscription in your App Store account settings (Settings, your name, Subscriptions) at least 24 hours before it renews. On the yearly trial, cancel at least 24 hours before the 7 days end and you won't be charged. Lifetime is a single payment with nothing to cancel. To get Pro back on a new or reset iPhone, open CoupleBites Pro in Settings and tap Restore Purchases.

What happens when I get a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID and install CoupleBites. Your diary, profile and shared diary come back from iCloud. For extra peace of mind, Manual backup in Settings exports your whole diary (meals with photos and places, your profile, the slime, your day together and places to try) to a file you can import on any iPhone. Reactions live only in iCloud.

Is there a Home Screen widget?

Yes. The Your slime widget, free for everyone, shows how your slime is doing and your days together. Pro adds Your partner's plate, with their latest meal, and a days together counter widget.
