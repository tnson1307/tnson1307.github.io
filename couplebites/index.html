What CoupleBites does

  • 📸

    Snap a photoEvery meal is automatically sorted as food or drink and turned into a clean sticker — no typing required.

  • 🔥

    Keep a streakA Duolingo-style daily streak, with freezes that forgive the occasional missed day.

  • 💌

    Share the momentScan a QR code to send a meal straight to your partner's phone — a direct connection between your two devices, no internet required.

🔒

Private by designCoupleBites has no server and no account. Everything you log stays on your own device — nothing about your meals is ever collected or seen by us.
